# Job Source Record Schema

## Identity

- source_identity
- company
- url
- source_type

Values:

- official
- campus
- third_party
- unknown

## Capture

- captured_at
- adapter_name
- extraction_method

## Verification

- status
- authority_level
- freshness

## Job Reference

- external_job_id
- role
- location
- department

## Boundary

Job Source Record is evidence input.

It does not contain:

- final fit decision
- application decision
- human approval
