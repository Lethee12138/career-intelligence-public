# Job Candidate Routing Schema

## Input

Job Scan Candidate

## Routing Factors

Keep separate:

- eligibility
- capability_fit
- preference_fit
- job_quality
- process_risk

## Allowed Outcomes

- OPPORTUNITY_POOL
- FAST_LANE
- TARGETED_PREPARE
- WATCH
- CLOSE

## Restrictions

Routing does not equal application approval.

External actions require Human Review.
