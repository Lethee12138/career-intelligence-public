# Interview preparation methods

Human-reviewed method update: 2026-09-19.

These methods extend the existing Career Intelligence preparation flow. They do not change role direction, create a new application process or guarantee interview outcomes.

## Company interview research dossier

When a role reaches PREPARE and interview preparation is relevant, build a bounded company dossier in addition to ordinary question practice.

Internal/company-side research:
- company business and products
- current public priorities and important recent actions
- commercial and product strategy where public evidence exists
- company values and stated operating principles
- evidence-backed hypotheses for why this role exists now

External/context research:
- core competitors
- relevant industry changes
- user or market changes
- competitive landscape

Evidence priority:
1. official recruitment page, corporate website and official accounts
2. company public materials
3. high-quality industry reporting, interviews and reports
4. social media and interview-experience reports
5. AI-assisted synthesis

AI-generated company facts are never authority. Verify consequential facts against the strongest available source and keep inference labelled as inference.

The final dossier should help answer:
- What problem might this team currently be solving?
- Why might this role be hiring now?
- Which verified candidate Evidence corresponds to that problem?
- Where could AI / Human-AI realistically enter the work process?

## Short-term AI Product / Agent preparation template

Use only for an AI Product, Agent or Human-AI Workflow role that is already worth preparing for.

1. Reverse-engineer the specific JD into likely high-frequency knowledge areas.
2. Fill only the necessary foundations, including where relevant:
   - Prompt
   - RAG
   - Agent
   - model evaluation
   - Bad Case analysis
   - MVP
   - cost / effect evaluation
3. Practice concrete cases instead of memorising definitions:
   - diagnose why an AI product is underperforming
   - investigate RAG recall problems
   - decompose an Agent product
4. Return to verified personal projects using:
   business context → user need → solution → trade-off → testing → problem → change → result.
5. Practice aloud with follow-up questions. Do not memorise a complete standard answer.

This is a concentrated preparation method, not a promised timeline or a guarantee of an offer.
