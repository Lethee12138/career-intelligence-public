# Dated market calibration v0.1.3

Use the public [market-calibration README](../references/market-calibration-v01/README.md), [salary CSV](../references/market-calibration-v01/Salary_Calibration_Matrix_v0.1.csv), [benefits CSV](../references/market-calibration-v01/Benefits_Employment_Conditions_Matrix_v0.1.csv), and [manifest](../references/market-calibration-v01/manifest.json) only as dated directional demo context when a specific market comparison is useful.

## Three layers

Durable method: Personal Preference ≠ Market Benchmark ≠ Candidate Evidence. Market salary quality ≠ immigration salary gate. Fixed base ≠ guaranteed cash ≠ variable compensation. Sponsor Licence ≠ Role Sponsorship. AI title does not establish AI responsibility or a salary premium. Company brand does not prove culture/workload. Currency conversion alone cannot establish offer quality. Current authoritative role evidence overrides dated generic context; use existing source and qualification rules.

Dated data: retain the public CSV fields, capture dates, confidence, derivation and stated limitations. Public rows are directional examples, not a statistical sample. FULL means retained traceability, not representative sampling or statistical certainty. PARTIAL permits directional context and verification questions only. Neither can automatically Apply / Skip / Reject or establish percentiles. Display confidence, traceability, capture date and material limitations with decision-relevant comparisons. Never fabricate missing sample counts or URLs. Benefits lack row-level confidence and trace audit: mark UNKNOWN_NOT_SUPPLIED / NOT_ASSESSED, not HIGH or FULL. Country-specific cells remain separate; compare actual economic, lifestyle and career value, never benefit-label counts.

Volatile facts: captured visa thresholds, going rates, register entries, Graduate visa rules, subsidies, bonus/hybrid policy and openings remain historical VERIFY-only evidence. Do not put their captured values in durable logic. A real-role conclusion requires new, exact-role, applicable authoritative verification. Research capture date is not last_verified for a candidate or role.

## Freshness and matching

No universal TTL. Salary/rent/recruiting context needs recruiting-cycle or meaningful-age review with explicit reviewer decision/reason and as-of date. Without that review, output RECHECK_REQUIRED; stale data may be shown as HISTORICAL_ONLY, never silently current. Visa/employer policy requires ROLE_LEVEL_FRESH_VERIFY regardless of report age. Structural methods remain durable unless policy changes. Statutory benefits still require current jurisdiction/applicability verification when used to assert rights.

Match market × city × route × responsibility-grounded role_family and fixed-base currency/period/gross basis. Do not infer route or family from title alone. Sparse Product Prototyping stays INSUFFICIENT_REFERENCE_DATA; retain any adjacent figures in the raw research but do not substitute them as exact family bands. Compare actual official role base to the common_normal_observed interval only as BELOW_DIRECTIONAL_BAND / WITHIN_DIRECTIONAL_BAND / ABOVE_DIRECTIONAL_BAND / INSUFFICIENT_REFERENCE_DATA. This is not a percentile or overall offer-quality grade. Preserve actual base and its source; the reference never overwrites it. Missing actual pay, incompatible scope/basis or unsupported ranges require insufficient data.

## Workflow use

/discover: context can identify possible compensation classes and weak data, never discard hypotheses because a dated band is low or imply current demand/openings. Calibration cannot become capability evidence.

/analyse-job: role-specific facts first; dated comparison second, including freshness and uncertainty. High salary does not erase workload/manager risks or qualification failure; apply existing Job Quality and eligibility gates separately.

/prepare-application: carry decision-changing concerns and bounded future HR questions; do not change factual CV claims, bypass readiness or automate negotiation.

UK six gates: current candidate right; sponsor licence; exact role/programme sponsorship; occupation code/duties; salary eligibility; timing. Record scope, source, verified_at and applicability for each; licence alone never passes role sponsorship. Salary market context cannot resolve any visa gate. No captured legal threshold is executed by the helper; a supplied fresh gate result still concerns that path only and other lawful routes stay separate.

Treat statutory leave and market prevalence as role- and jurisdiction-specific verification items. A public benchmark cannot rewrite a caller's personal preference or establish a legal entitlement.

Optional [calibration guard](../scripts/market_calibration_guard.py) reads the local references and checks explicit reviewed inputs. It does not fetch, authenticate sources, infer current law, rank offers or replace Core gates.
