# Discovery rules — Slice 2

Apply the existing [evidence](evidence.md), [source hierarchy](source-and-inference.md), [eligibility](eligibility.md), [assessment](opportunity-assessment.md), [questioning](user-questioning.md) and [claims](cv-claims.md) rules unchanged. Discovery is a Human Review candidate, never automatic profile growth or a CV claim.

## Capability epistemics

SELF_IDENTIFIED means an explicit user claim, not verification. DEMONSTRATED requires directly inspected, verified evidence of the bounded capability. INFERRED means a traceable behavioral inference: prefer two or more materially distinct contexts, or one unusually strong record with clear ownership, decision, execution and result. Explain the shared behavior; counting projects or repeated copies of one source is insufficient. POTENTIAL retains candidate-attributable weak signals without a stable capability assertion. A result explicitly owned by another team member is not even a positive weak signal of the candidate's capability; record it as unsupported/missing evidence, not POTENTIAL. These are epistemic statuses, not skill levels. Confidence is STRONG / MODERATE / TENTATIVE, never numeric.

Before adding a capability inspect existing profile/evidence and deduplicate by exact source identity and bounded behavior. Similar labels alone do not establish identity; ambiguous overlap remains a review question. Preserve direct-user, assistant/report and mixed provenance. Inspected secondary reports can support a labelled inference, not a DEMONSTRATED promotion. A tool, course, syllabus, polished CV phrase, AI-generated implementation or team outcome with unclear ownership cannot alone establish stable capability. Never enrich personal truth from generic web content or scan all public profiles by default.

Record evidenceRefs, contexts, repeated behavior, ownership strength and boundary, limitations and counterevidence for every inference. Cross-reference similar experiences without merging their ownership. Missing ownership prevents Route C; retain POTENTIAL and a bounded verification need. An explicit self-claim may enter Route B but its role premise must remain unverified.

## Outputs before titles

Inspect completed outputs and cutoff first. For each record distinguish working / interactive / playable / concept-only status; research, framing, workflow/system logic, product decisions, prototype, AI-assisted implementation, tests and iteration. Preserve Human / AI / team contributions per phase; UNKNOWN is preferable to assigning the whole project to the candidate. Exclude ongoing work when the batch requires completed evidence.

Ask which real responsibilities produce, validate or own similar outputs. AI-assisted prototype does not establish Software Engineering or front-end development; a UX/course project does not establish high-craft UX/UI design; research does not establish a pure research occupation. Output likeness generates hypotheses, not job-market facts.

## Role and market boundaries

Seed roles must receive supports, counters, misconceptions, gaps, alternatives and confirm/weaken conditions. Do not optimize to prove the original choice. Degree is context, not a discovery filter. Industry is OPEN by default; provide multiple plausible industries/team types per hypothesis. Remove only for explicit exclusion, hard qualification mismatch, repeated work-style mismatch or verified market impossibility, citing the reason and scope.

Discovery is high-recall opportunity generation; selection is the later qualification, fit, Job Quality, practical-fit and evidence screen. Expand from the complete set of relevant capability roots, not only the newest evidence or current seed hypotheses. A few strong openings do not complete discovery. Include grounded combinations across Product/Digital Product, Research/Insights/CX, Systems/Workflow/Product Ops, Transformation/Business Improvement, Strategy/Innovation, Consumer/Customer/Audience, media/communication-derived digital work, Content/Creator Product, Editorial/Media Innovation, Product/Technology Communications where early-career realistic, Creative Technology, AI-enabled Product/Workflow, AI Evaluation/HITL and selective Agent/AI Product, plus other evidence-derived problem-first families. These are directions, not a whitelist or equal-quota claim.

`AI_NEUTRAL_BY_DEFAULT`: record AI involvement as NONE, AI-ENABLED, AI-CORE or AGENT-CORE. AI is a job attribute, never a discovery admission gate or priority bonus. Human-AI or AI-assisted evidence must not narrow the universe to AI roles. A non-AI role may lead when capability, evidence, Job Quality, practical fit or sustainable Career Value is stronger. Conversely, AI/Agent wording never supplies RAG, SQL, Python, architecture, deployment or Agent ownership evidence.

`AI_ENABLED_MIDDLE_LANE_REQUIRED`: do not collapse discovery into a binary of pure-AI roles versus roles with no AI. Always inspect the middle lane where the role family remains Product, Research/Insights, Product Ops/Workflow, Transformation, Strategy/Innovation, Content/Creator Product, CX/Experience or adjacent digital work, while AI is embedded in product features, user experience, research/analysis methods, internal workflows, decision support, content systems or operational processes. This middle lane may be the strongest fit and must receive explicit coverage in discovery and concentration audits. It is distinct from AI-CORE/AGENT-CORE technical ownership and distinct from NONE.

Media and Communication education, audience understanding, content/narrative thinking, communication, media/creator context and confirmed media experiences participate as capability roots in the same hidden-capability and problem-first routes. Seek cross-domain duties across media/communication × digital/product/research/audience/content/technology/workflow. Do not create a separate media lane; neither traditional media nor AI-media receives automatic priority.

Every discovery report declares capability_roots_considered, coverage gaps and a stop reason. Audit concentration across role family, industry, company, city/market, AI involvement and source accessibility. A concentrated returned sample is `COVERAGE_BIAS_DETECTED`, not market distribution or `COVERAGE_SUFFICIENT`. Soft search budgets may broaden missing relevant areas but cannot fabricate market quotas.

Use duties rather than titles. Product Operations may own growth/revenue; AI Transformation may be presales; AI Experience may require visual craft; Product Engineer may require production engineering. Mark these as title traps requiring duty-level verification, not universal definitions.

Use the shared work-style rule: core quota, acquisition, revenue, persuasive selling and negotiation-heavy client work are strong negatives. Stakeholder meetings, research interviews, cross-functional collaboration, internal presentations and normal explanation are neutral by default. Interview process risk remains separate. MT is not preferred by default: retain only with clear function, low sales/rotation risk and high Career Value. Prefer the direct functional route at similar value unless MT has unusually clear functional fit. UNKNOWN MT conditions do not pass this gate.

ANALYSIS_ONLY is a complete offline result: hypotheses, search strategies and validation questions, with no current openings fabricated. MARKET_VALIDATED requires supplied market observations, compares duties and records title variants and hard requirements. For VALIDATED_BY_CURRENT_JD require inspected current original authority, exact job identity, observation date/as-of and duty-level corroboration. It validates that job's work hypothesis, not candidate eligibility, the whole family or permission to apply. MARKET_SIGNAL_FOUND may describe secondary/historical signals with their date and scope. Taxonomy alone leaves UNVALIDATED.

CONTRADICTED must name the exact job/version and duty or hard mismatch, its source strength and historical/current scope. Preserve the original hypothesis and append the observation; never erase a whole family because one JD requires STEM. Historical reported contradictions remain historical and require original/current verification. Delegate formal qualification and recommendations to /analyse-job; preparation stays behind /prepare-application. A hypothesis is not a recommendation before market validation; a market state alone never supplies candidate eligibility or Human acceptance. DEFERRED records a deliberate pause and reason.

ESCO / O*NET are optional REFERENCE VOCABULARY for title variants and occupation sanity checks only. They cannot prove capability, hiring, China demand or eligibility. No datasets, APIs or runtime dependency.

## Bounded questioning

Read existing evidence first, then a broad question only if material; selectively deepen the answer that changes capability status, ownership, role hypothesis, preference or market feasibility. Stop on repeated evidence, two successive answers with no new decision signal, inaccessible information, cosmetic completeness, or JD-specific gaps with no relevant JD. No default tests, personality interview, mandatory story for every gap or job-count-based confidence. Record why questioning stopped. Do not repeat every rule in the final report; show the consequential boundary once beside the affected conclusion.
