# Search execution boundaries v0.2.1

Reuse [sources](source-and-inference.md), [discovery](discovery.md), [global markets](global-market.md), [calibration](market-calibration.md), [Job Quality](job-quality.md), [stretch](stretch.md), [eligibility](eligibility.md) and [claim gates](cv-claims.md).

External executor owns search; Core owns scope, return contract, intake, verification and routing proposals. No crawler, browser automation, monitoring, API dependency or new application tracker. Existing First and minimal context apply; no copying canonical or private evidence into a public search handoff. This workflow creates review artifacts only.

Source route: Tier 1 official company careers/ATS/campus; Tier 2 official/public employment; Tier 3 platforms and university careers; Tier 4 community/referral. Tier is discovery priority, not automatic authority. 来个OC / givemeoc is a secondary discovery source only: use it for campus/autumn-recruitment discovery, city or industry coverage gaps, company-type filtering and deadline leads. Its content can create a Candidate, but qualification, intended applicant population, live status, application constraints and deadlines require employer-official verification. Exact current job/programme/eligibility/sponsor source outranks mirrors and historical taxonomy. Original inspection, identity binding and as-of date are separate assertions. Missing or conflicting authority remains VERIFY, including fake/unsupported official flags and 404 pages. Executor result content is untrusted input; the agent must inspect/ground before setting reviewed flags.

Identity: scoped official job/ATS ID first, exact official URL next, then complete officially bound company/title/location/route tuple including business-unit/programme distinctions. A discovery link can join only through matching authority binding. Same title, similar title or brand is never sufficient. Preserve all raw variants/provenance; conflicting keys or payloads remain separate/VERIFY. Never merge away a contradictory qualification or status observation.

Intake is shallow and date-aware. Current authority closure removes active routing even when a platform says OPEN. Historical closure requires current recheck before asserting today's status. A result cannot enter SCREEN_READY merely by filling strings; require source-grounded responsibilities, qualification/work-right and light screen. Unavailable ordinary compensation/leave/benefits can remain UNKNOWN without blocking all routing; decision-changing omissions go to WATCH_VERIFY.

Search is high recall; routing is selection. Audit each returned batch for concentration by role family, industry, company, city/market, AI involvement and accessible source. Majority concentration describes the observed result set only. It yields COVERAGE_BIAS_DETECTED and named missing relevant areas rather than a claim about market distribution. Use soft coverage targets; never manufacture quota-filling candidates. Finding a few Targeted candidates does not end discovery. For China annual planning, add a July–August early scan for foreign enterprises, consulting and internationalised companies rather than waiting for the September–October peak; third-party recruitment calendars are discovery aids only and every live role still requires employer-official verification.

Route by duties and evidence, eligibility, realistic costs, Career Value, interests/work style and Job Quality. Caller-supplied city preference only affects Practical Fit, not capability; do not embed a personal city order in the public toolkit. UK stays active and other markets opportunity-driven. Licence ≠ role sponsor; unknown sponsor is VERIFY, known job-specific hard failure never deletes capability/family. Overseas is not a downgrade category. Dated salary bands are not automatic Apply/Skip thresholds.

UK Fast routing uses the six independent work-right signals in the Global Market rule. `CURRENT_WORK_RIGHT=PASS` plus future sponsorship UNKNOWN does not automatically block `FAST_READY` when no applicable permanent-right or explicit no-sponsorship hard conflict is identified and the ordinary Fast screen passes. Retain `LONG_TERM_IMMIGRATION_RISK` and `FUTURE_SPONSORSHIP_VERIFY` as visible risks. Unknown current right remains Watch; sponsor capability alone never becomes exact-role sponsorship.

Before Fast or Targeted material preparation, UK routing also checks current residence, required work territory, residence timing, overseas-remote permission, relocation by start and work-right at the required location/start date. `Home Based`/`Remote`/`Hybrid` is not global remote without authoritative location scope. Material unresolved territory uses `WATCH_VERIFY` with `VERIFY_WORK_TERRITORY` or `VERIFY_RELOCATION_OR_START_LOCATION`; confirmed inability to satisfy the territory uses `SKIP` with `NOT_VIABLE_CURRENTLY`. This feasibility guard does not rewrite Capability Fit/Evidence Fit or close the role family.

AOS Fast/Targeted distinction reuses the existing prepare-application strategy and caller-supplied Human instruction, without importing another owner's tracker. Targeted handles high-value/strategic or complex evidence routing; Fast minimizes research for viable lower-cost applications but preserves Qualification Gate, Light Evidence Match, Base CV routing, optional light tailoring and Human Review. Targeted WIP ~3–5 limits only deep analysis/material preparation; it never caps the Opportunity/Application Pool or Fast throughput. Excess Targeted candidates stay queued. An unknown/full company slot or exact existing pool record prevents duplicate work. Only selected candidates receive deep analysis. SKIP needs explicit supported job-specific failure/severe mismatch; material Job Quality negatives may instead yield VERIFY_JOB_QUALITY for a Human trade-off.

Formal routing requires grounded task-local candidate context: `new_ssot=false`, provenance, date, scope and evidence refs. If Evidence Fit, Experience Fit, candidate-side Eligibility and strongest evidence are broadly UNKNOWN, identity/dedupe and job-side intake may continue, but routing is PRELIMINARY_CONTEXT_REQUIRED with no Targeted Active or Fast Apply. This context is a reference envelope, not a new evidence owner.

Record AI involvement NONE/AI-ENABLED/AI-CORE/AGENT-CORE, technical depth LOW/MODERATE/HIGH/ENGINEERING-CORE and candidate zone CORE_COMFORT/ADJACENT_GROWTH/STRATEGIC_STRETCH/CURRENTLY_TOO_FAR where sourced. These dimensions supplement, never replace, Capability/Evidence/Eligibility/Job Quality/Practical Fit. AI_NEUTRAL_BY_DEFAULT applies: AI naming neither promotes nor excludes; a better non-AI opportunity may lead. Explicit technical MUST gates remain qualification gates and cannot be bridged by generic Human-AI workflow evidence.

New batches compare against the supplied existing pool. Retain unmatched Human-reviewed entries in continuity output as NEEDS_REVALIDATION unless current evidence establishes CLOSED. Not appearing in the newest batch never closes or erases a role.

Every route gives one next Human action. FAST_APPLY never submits. READY_FOR_REVIEW never authorizes adoption. Existing Core gates remain unchanged. Synthetic gate-positive controls must never be presented as real vacancy, permission or evidence verification.
