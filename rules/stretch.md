# No Invented Facts; Evidence-Anchored Potential Stretch; Strategic Packaging

Human-approved Core Integration increment. Reuse [evidence](evidence.md), [claims](cv-claims.md), [discovery](discovery.md) and [shared contracts](../schemas/contracts.md). No new evidence owner, capability status or application gate.

## Independent claim/use boundary

Keep SELF_IDENTIFIED / DEMONSTRATED / INFERRED / POTENTIAL unchanged. Add `use_boundary` to an individual claim or intended use, independently of capability epistemic status:

- VERIFIED_FACT: the current input Evidence directly supports the bounded proposition at the cited source/version; this does not imply independent real-world verification. Verification of what a report says is only a documentary fact; it does not verify the reported personal achievement or live behavior. State verification_scope.
- DEFENSIBLE_STRETCH: an explainable adjacent transfer from grounded behavior. It can support positioning, role hypotheses, fit discussion, careful resume framing, interview bridges, project selection and recruiter concern handling. It does not become a fact or SUPPORTED match.
- SPECULATIVE_UNSUPPORTED: insufficient transfer bridge; exclude from affirmative packaging. Missing evidence is not proof of permanent inability.

These are use boundaries, not confidence, skill levels, material-use permission or Human approval. The same evidence may support a narrow VERIFIED_FACT and a broader DEFENSIBLE_STRETCH; keep separate claim IDs. A report-derived inference remains INFERENCE even when its use is defensible. No numeric confidence.

## Defensible stretch test

Record source capability IDs/evidence refs, target capability/responsibility, bridge reasoning, what remains unproven, allowed uses and prohibited overclaim. Prefer adjacency to directly demonstrated behavior and repeated material contexts where practical. If direct originals are unavailable, a traceable report-derived INFERRED pattern may support conditional framing review; disclose that grounding rather than pretending it is directly verified. A strong single record may suffice with explicit ownership, decision, execution and result. Mere tool/course/title resemblance does not.

Review adjacency, repetition/strong-single rationale, counterevidence, ownership clarity, transfer mechanism, credentials and interviewer defensibility. Do not convert this qualitative review into a majority score: unclear material ownership, direct contradictory evidence, invented hard facts/credentials or an unexplained bridge block that stretch. Missing evidence is an unproven boundary, not automatically a direct contradiction. Prior PM employment is not required for product potential.

Before resume/interview framing, answer internally: L1 What was actually done, with refs and personal/team/AI scope? L2 Why does that behavior transfer to the target responsibility? L3 What has not been done or proven? All three need substantive reviewed answers. If they fail, do not use strong stretch wording. They need not appear in every CV bullet; keep material truth boundaries in internal analysis and interview notes. Select the strongest truthful wording and ownership verbs supported by the source. Omit irrelevant caveats from the outward sentence without hiding a material factual distinction.

## Hard facts cannot stretch

No factual expansion of company/role identity, metric, number, date, employment/status, official title, degree/qualification, certification, production deployment, commercial impact, user/client adoption, team size, participant count, formal technical-stack usage, formal ownership or direct management responsibility. Preserve exact quantities and scope: 9+ documented changes is not a percent improvement or participant count. Prototype implementation ownership is not whole-project leadership. Human-AI workflow and AI-assisted build are not ML model development, RAG implementation, backend/full-stack engineering or formal technical expertise without extra evidence.

## Four-workflow continuity

/position actively surfaces adjacent/emerging/transferable potential with separate epistemic and use boundaries. Do not interpret missing prior job title or direct proof as negative ability evidence.

/discover uses DEFENSIBLE_STRETCH to widen responsibility hypotheses and search vocabulary, preserving source capability and route lineage. It does not promote POTENTIAL to INFERRED just to enter Route C; a potential target can be reached from a qualified source INFERRED pattern via its bridge. Hypotheses still require market validation; stretch creates no current opening or employer fact.

/analyse-job distinguishes proven bounded support, transferable/stretch fit and unsupported gaps. An adjacent bridge yields PARTIAL + DEFENSIBLE_STRETCH with the unproven portion visible; never SUPPORTED solely from stretch. No bridge yields UNSUPPORTED / SPECULATIVE_UNSUPPORTED after adequate bounded review; inadequate access may instead require UNKNOWN. Qualification, market authority and recommendation gates remain unchanged.

/prepare-application actively strengthens truthful wording, selects relevant projects and explains recruiter-facing value. Verified facts use the existing claim chain; stretch adds source capability → bridge → target framing, retaining verified premises where available. Unknown grounding or permission is not cured by wording. For an explicitly requested historical integration review only, PREPARATION_HOLD may include a separate `framing_review`: internal conditional wording comparisons and L1–L3 notes, marked NOT_FOR_ADOPTION. Keep `claim_candidates=[]`, no packet, no canonical CV, no application readiness. This narrow review allowance does not change the live preparation gate or default HOLD output. Actual resume/public use still requires source/permission checks and Human review.
