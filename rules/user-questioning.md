# Selective questioning

Before asking, inspect canonical, relevant existing evidence, Job Record and CV/Field Bank references. Record which sources/sections were checked and inaccessible. Do not re-interview the whole career.

For each proposed question state: unresolved decision, existing evidence checked, possible answers and how they could change eligibility, priority, claim safety or preparation route. Ask only if one answer materially changes that decision. Batch independent questions briefly; prefer the single highest-impact blocker first. Stop when the route is stable, answers repeat known facts, the detail is cosmetic, evidence is unavailable or further questioning has low information gain. Retain UNKNOWN and state its effect.

Use selective deep dive for a particular strong claim, ambiguity or interviewer objection. Interview depth: event/action → why/tradeoff → evidence/limit. Do not require a failure story, precise metric, full package or public demo just to fill a template. New details are review candidates; classify metric corrections versus new snapshots versus scope clarification versus later development. No automatic evidence writeback.
