# Qualification independent of capability

Classify each JD requirement from exact wording and scope as MUST, STRONG PREFERENCE, NICE TO HAVE or UNKNOWN / AMBIGUOUS. Quote or precisely locate the wording. A preferred major, internship or tool is not a hard gate. Degree does not constrain role families unless the actual role imposes a verified mandatory qualification.

Check applicable hard qualifications separately: graduation/award window (including overseas certificate rules), degree, mandatory major, experience threshold, work right/sponsorship, location/onsite conditions, start date, internship/conversion requirements. Do not assume all apply; explicit NOT_APPLICABLE needs a reason and source. An uncertain award interval is eligible for a date gate only when the entire interval fits; overlap alone is VERIFY.

- ELIGIBLE: all applicable hard gates supported; label scope and remaining noncritical unknowns.
- NOT ELIGIBLE: at least one explicit verified hard gate fails; retain supporting capability evidence.
- VERIFY: any applicable hard gate/source applicability is ambiguous or missing.

Graduation-window ELIGIBLE alone is not overall ELIGIBLE. A preferred technical degree gap remains visible in Experience/Domain/Evidence dimensions; it does not become NOT ELIGIBLE. Strong capability cannot override hard eligibility failure. Never label a qualified person capability-fit merely because they are qualified.

For market-specific work-right/sponsorship states and distinct employment-market/company/location fields, apply [global-market rules](global-market.md). A job-specific legal/eligibility failure never negates a capability or permanently excludes its market.
