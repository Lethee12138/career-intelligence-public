# /discover

Goal: capability/problem/output evidence → plausible responsibilities → role hypotheses → search strategies → supplied-market validation. Does not automatically search, scrape or require exact titles.

1. Load [discovery rules](../rules/discovery.md), [role contract](../schemas/role-hypothesis.md), existing profile or run [/position](position.md). State ANALYSIS_ONLY unless market observations are supplied; historical signals must remain historical even in MARKET_VALIDATED mode. No web access is required.
2. Inventory all relevant capability roots before selecting routes; recent or visually salient Human-AI work does not replace education, audience/content/communication, research, product, systems, transformation or other grounded roots. Execute all applicable routes and name why a route lacks input rather than fabricating it:
   - A Seed-role: retrieve why considered; seek supporting AND counter evidence, adjacent roles and market contradictions.
   - B Explicit capability: DEMONSTRATED or SELF_IDENTIFIED → concrete work problem → owning team → role family. Self-identified premises remain unverified.
   - C Hidden capability: traceable INFERRED behavior with sufficient bounded ownership → plausible use in a team → role. POTENTIAL/unclear ownership does not qualify.
   - D Problem-first: repeated solved problems → teams owning them → actual responsibilities → market search terms. This route works without a seed or known title.
3. Apply the Output-first lens across routes: compare responsibilities to completed interactive/working/playable outputs and candidate decisions, tests and iteration. Explain Human/AI/team boundaries. Deduplicate role hypotheses only when responsibility scope agrees; preserve distinct variants and route lineage.
4. For each hypothesis provide core/adjacent titles, responsibility/problem/output keywords, negative terms/title traps and multiple industry/team categories. Apply duty-level title traps and work-style/MT rules. Degree alone never excludes the family. Terms such as agent experience are vocabulary, not proof of Agent/RAG expertise.
5. For supplied market evidence compare actual duties, title variants and MUST vs preferred requirements. Append a scoped validation observation; preserve hypothesis history. Current original JD corroboration can yield VALIDATED_BY_CURRENT_JD; historical reports yield scoped signals or reported contradiction, never invented current vacancies. Taxonomy supplies no validation. A hard mismatch contradicts the exact job/version; send it to [/analyse-job](analyse-job.md) for formal qualification. Conflicting or insufficient evidence stays unresolved, not averaged into a score.
6. Return a readable discovery report with counters beside hypotheses, AI involvement, capability-root coverage, concentration gaps, validation plan, and a small next step the Human can choose. Discovery stays high recall; selection happens downstream. Do not stop merely because 2–3 strong hypotheses or Targeted candidates exist. ANALYSIS_ONLY ends with hypotheses/search strategies/questions for future JD inspection, not application recommendations. MARKET_VALIDATED reports what changed and why, without bypassing Slice 1 gates. No searches, submissions, CV edits, new projects or automatic profile writeback. Stop questioning when information gain ends.

Core Integration: apply [stretch policy](../rules/stretch.md) to expand plausible responsibilities through explicit bridges from source capabilities. Preserve epistemic status, route lineage, counterevidence and market validation requirements.

Job Quality v0.1.1: apply [job-quality rules](../rules/job-quality.md). Use sourced job-quality signals to adjust pursuit priority, retaining capability adjacency. Unknown pay/team conditions require verification, not title/brand stereotypes.

Global Market v0.1.2: follow [global-market rules](../rules/global-market.md). Generate responsibility hypotheses first, then China/UK/international title variants and specific market checks; UK stays active and other markets remain opportunity-driven.

Use [dated calibration](../rules/market-calibration.md) only as market context; retain role hypotheses and capability evidence independently.

For an explicitly requested search execution plan, pass the existing hypotheses and references to [/find-jobs](find-jobs.md). This does not authorize external search or make taxonomy current market evidence.
