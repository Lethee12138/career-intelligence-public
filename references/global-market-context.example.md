# Synthetic Global Market Context Example

This is a non-personal example. It does not describe a real candidate's work-right, location, salary or relocation status.

- China: active execution market in this example.
- UK: active opportunity market in this example.
- Other markets: opportunity-driven and role-specific.
- Candidate authorization, sponsorship need, employer capability and exact-role sponsorship remain separate fields.
- Unknown work-right or sponsorship remains `UNKNOWN` / `VERIFY`.

Supply current, role-specific authority evidence separately when evaluating a real opportunity.
